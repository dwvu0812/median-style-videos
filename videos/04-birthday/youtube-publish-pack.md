Why 23 People Is Enough — The Birthday Paradox

## Description
In a room of just 23 people, it is more likely than not that two of them share a birthday. Most people guess around 180. The real answer is 23 — and the reason is not magic, it is pairs: 23 people make 253 pairs, and every pair is a chance.

Your brain asks the wrong question. It asks: will someone share MY birthday? The maths asks a different one: does ANY two people share a birthday? That is the whole paradox.
