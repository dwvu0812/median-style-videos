# Video 03 — "Why GPS Would Fail in 2 Minutes Without Einstein"

**Series:** Median-style animated explainers, English, for an international YouTube audience.
**Core idea (one sentence):** Your phone finds where you are by measuring time to billionths of a second — and Einstein's relativity makes satellite clocks drift by 38 microseconds a day, which is 11 km of error a day, 8 metres a minute, so after 2 minutes GPS accuracy is already broken unless engineers pre-correct the clocks.
**Through-line (carried through the whole video):** "Your phone would lie to you by 11 km a day — unless Einstein saves it."
**Format:** long-form, 38 spoken lines, 38 scenes, 8 chapters. **v2.0: 1,479 words, raw 616.7s (10:16), sped up 1.25x → ~493s (~8:13) fast final. v2.2: raw 621.32s (10:21), sped up 1.25x → ~497s (~8:17) fast final** (4 lines re-recorded: +4.632s net). **v2.3: raw 765.248s (12:45), sped up 1.25x → 612.2s (10:12) fast final** (voice B, slower pace). **v2.4: NO speedup — final is the raw 1.0x render, 765.28s (12:45), voice exactly as sampled** (user: the 1.25x made the audio sound rushed and warped unlike the samples).
**Voice:** v2.3: Muse built-in TTS **avocado_v2:vd2_quivering_oak** ("voice B", very deep British, slow pacing), English, **speed 80**. Chosen by the user 2026-11-26 after listening to 4 samples (Magnus / Quivering Oak / Trusty Beam / voice A slowed). v2.2 and earlier used voice A `vd2_smoky_teacup` speed 105 — Vietnamese accent, and the user said it sounded rushed and unnatural: measured cause was ~200 wpm effective (TTS 105 × 1.25x video speedup). ## Style (true Median, verified from real videos)
Pure black #000, flat 2D line art, white #FFF main, grey #9A9A9A secondary, accents only periwinkle #7E9BFF (selected/correction) and mint #6FCF97 (question) used sparingly — v2.0 uses them boldly at chapter peaks (giant numbers, underlines, pulses). Serif maths (Times/Georgia), bold sans headings, small tracked-caps grey eyebrow ("PART 1", "PART 2", … "RECAP" — English, not "PHẦN"). One centred object, huge negative space, slow fades, no camera moves, no 3D/glow/emoji. Scenes crossfade 0.45s, blur on the outgoing scene only (max 3px), incoming sharp (never display:none); gap bug fix: during pauses between lines keep the current scene. Motion (v2.2): slow blur-in entrances 1.8s ease-out cubic-bezier(0.22,1,0.36,1), line draws 2.5s ease-in-out, gentle 5–6s breathing loops, at most 2 things moving at once, one element at a time staggered 0.8s; orbits and clock hands stay linear (constant speed); scene clock from performance.now() wall time (NOT the audio element's currentTime — the audio clock runs ~2% slow under recording load). CSS keyframes (spin/drift/bob/breathe/pulse/grow-loop/sway/arrive) scoped under `.active`, plus SMIL animateMotion for travelling dots/pulses; one JS count-up (scene 23, "38" counts 0→38, 3s ease-out).
